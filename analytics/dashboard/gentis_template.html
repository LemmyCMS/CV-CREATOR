Target attainment

The pattern, not any single row, is the finding: calls land at 93–99% of target and everything downstream lands at 7–32%. A team hitting its dialling numbers and missing everything they were supposed to produce is not a motivation problem — it is a plan whose conversion assumptions were never true.
